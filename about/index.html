About Thai

Built inside the work, not outside it.

Thai Biazzini has spent six years across agency, account growth, brand strategy, content, campaigns and purpose-led marketing.

Across real estate, hospitality, personal brands and social-impact work, the pattern stayed the same: the strongest results appeared when the right story reached people who already had a reason to care.

NARA Studio was built around that lesson.

Point of view

Personal brand is not a vanity layer added after the sales system.

For founder-led businesses, it is the trust layer that helps the market understand the offer, believe the proof and feel comfortable starting a conversation.

The work should still be measured commercially.

What Thai brings

Strategy, activation and proof in one system.

  • brand and campaign direction;
  • direct audience activation;
  • founder interviewing and story extraction;
  • proof-led content systems;
  • cross-channel execution; and
  • English, Portuguese and Spanish communication.
How NARA works

The founder stays in the commercial conversation.

Thai owns strategy, voice, creative direction and the quality of the market interaction. Ben supports offer architecture, systems, CRM and commercial measurement where required.

The client brings the expertise, proof, relationships and sales conversations.
